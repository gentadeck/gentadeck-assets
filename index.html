Before you buy

Please read these notes so the template works exactly as you expect.

Photos are not included

Preview images use stock photos for demonstration only. The picture placeholders in the file are empty, so you add your own images.

Made for Microsoft PowerPoint

Works with PowerPoint 2019 and Microsoft 365 on Windows and Mac. Google Slides, Keynote and Canva are not supported and may display differently.

Install the fonts first

The ZIP includes Source Serif 4 and Source Sans 3. Install them before opening the file, or text and line breaks may shift.

Sample content only

All company names, people, numbers and charts are fictional. Replace them with your own data before presenting.

No animations or Morph

All slides are static. No animations, transitions or Morph effects are used, so nothing moves unexpectedly while you edit or present.

Digital download

No physical item is shipped. Use it for your own presentations and client work. Reselling or sharing the template is not allowed.
