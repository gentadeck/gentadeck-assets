No physical item is shipped. You receive a ZIP file with the PPTX, the font pack and a Read-me-first guide. If your download fails or the file does not open, email us and we will help you.

Preview images use stock photos for demonstration only. Photos are not included, and the picture placeholders are empty. All company names, people, numbers and charts are fictional, so replace them with your own data before presenting.
